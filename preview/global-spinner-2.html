Triangle tunnel · Dismiss
The nested red-gold-blue field holds on its final perspective frame until dismissed. The preview controls reset the cycle to Loading; a reusable integration can map the same transition to its dismiss lifecycle. Reduced-motion skips the tunnel motion and keeps the true-north mark static.
